The area

Why Square One

Square One Shopping Centre is the largest mall in Ontario, and the neighbourhood around it has turned into a genuine downtown — Celebration Square for events and skating, the Living Arts Centre, the Art Gallery of Mississauga, the Central Library, and Sheridan College’s downtown campus all within a few blocks.

The Hurontario corridor is the spine of it. The Hurontario LRT runs the length of the street, connecting Port Credit on the lake up through downtown toward Brampton, and the Cooksville GO mobility hub puts the Milton line and the Toronto core within reach without a car.

It is still Mississauga, so the practical stuff is close too: Whole Foods and Crate and Barrel on Square One Drive, Metro and FreshCo for the weekly shop, the 403 to the north, and the QEW and the lakeshore to the south.
