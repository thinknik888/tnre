About Alba

The podium from Hurontario Street

Alba is a 418-suite community by Edenshaw Developments at the corner of Hurontario Street and Fairview Road East, in Downtown Mississauga — a short walk from Square One. A 25-storey tower rises from a seven-storey black-and-white clad podium, with elegantly curved balconies and unobstructed views: Lake Ontario to the south, the Toronto skyline to the east, the Mississauga skyline to the north.

Designed with Core Architects and Cecconi Simone, the interiors take a Scandinavian direction — neutral tones, natural textures, warmth and quiet. Over 20,000 square feet of amenities spread across three floors, and the building runs on a geothermal system as a LEED Certified Candidate.

Suites run from a 427 sq ft one bedroom to an 863 sq ft two bedroom plus den. Ceilings are 9 ft on floors 3 to 30, and 10 ft on 31 and 32.
