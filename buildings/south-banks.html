Overview

About South Banks

South Banks is a new collection of coastal townhomes by DECO Homes and OPUS Homes inside Lakeview Village — the 177-acre master-planned waterfront community taking shape on Mississauga’s shoreline. Seventeen low-rise blocks sit between Aerodrome Avenue and Marina Vista, directly across from Waterway Common Park and a short walk from Lake Ontario.

The launch offers six plans at nine price points: single-storey Azure and Breeze homes with private terraces, and three-level Coast and Drift homes, some with a rooftop terrace. Every home is above grade with two full baths, an underground parking spot included, and a third or fourth bedroom option. Pricing starts at $549,990 net of the HST rebate, development levies are capped at $0, and occupancy is 2029.

The setting

Mornings on the water

Lakeview Village reopens a stretch of Mississauga shoreline that was off-limits for decades: 177 acres, 45 acres of parkland, a one-kilometre pier, and a waterfront trail that runs to Port Credit. South Banks sits one block from it.

Paddle before work, cycle the trail, or take Long Branch GO downtown in about 25 minutes — lakeside living with a city commute.
