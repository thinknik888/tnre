About Pier House Towns

Introducing Pier House, Branthaven’s townhome enclave inside the visionary Lakeview Village master plan — a 177-acre waterfront revitalization that will reshape Mississauga’s shoreline with new parks, trails, cultural amenities and conservation lands.

Four contemporary collections of family-sized, three-storey homes, each with outdoor space, an above-grade private garage and a driveway. One minute’s walk to the waterfront pier; a twenty-minute commute downtown via Long Branch GO, the TTC loop or the QEW.
