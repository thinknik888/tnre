About Aura Lakeview Village Towns

Aura sits a two-minute walk from the Lake Ontario waterfront inside Lakeview Village — Canada’s most transformative waterfront community, designed for healthier, future-ready living.

Two-storey urban towns in Main and Upper Residences, with underground parking, built-in retail and offices, and the lowest entry point in the GTA for new townhome living at the waterfront.
