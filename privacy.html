Privacy Policy

Last updated September 17, 2026

CondosAround.com is operated by Nikhil Oberoi, a licensed real estate broker in Ontario, Canada. This page explains what information we collect when you use this website or fill in one of our forms, including lead forms shown on Facebook and Instagram, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number and email address when you register for a price list or floor plans, save a floor plan, or contact us.
  • What you were interested in: the community or building you registered for, and the advertisement or link that brought you to us.
  • Basic usage information: pages visited on this site, collected through the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and standard server logs.

How we use it

  • To send you the price lists, floor plans and project information you asked for.
  • To contact you by phone, text message or email about the properties you enquired about and similar new-construction opportunities.
  • To understand which pages and advertisements are useful, and to show relevant ads on Facebook and Instagram.

Who sees it

Your details go to Nikhil Oberoi. We do not sell your information. It is stored with the service providers that run this website and its advertising (website hosting, and Meta Platforms for ads and lead forms), who process it only on our behalf. We may share your enquiry with a builder’s sales office when that is needed to register you for a project, and we will disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Call or text 647-924-0848 and we will take care of it. You can also reply STOP to any text message, or unsubscribe from any email.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as you are actively looking, or until you ask us to remove them.
